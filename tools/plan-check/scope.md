# Scope: where your issue lives, and the rules of the room

## Where your issue lives

Only issues in the course's Path Review repository are in scope this week:

- Repo: `codepath/pathreview-ai301-fa26-s1`

The plan must belong to the issue reproduced in Unit 2 or the instructor's
house issue.

## Path Review house rules

Path Review is a classroom, so classmates are not strangers:

- A classmate's plan does not block yours. Post your own plan anyway, in your
  own words and from your own reproduction.
- Never piggyback a plan with "same approach as above." Your diagnosis,
  scope, test plan, and comment must stand on their own.
- Branch on your own fork with `fix/<issue-number>-<slug>`, one branch per
  issue, and push the branch to your fork rather than the shared repository.
- Credit attaches to the plan, branch, and later pull request you post; being
  first is not the grading criterion.
