# Scope: where your issue lives, and the rules of the room

## Where your issue lives

Only issues in the course's Path Review repository are in scope this week:

- Repo: `codepath/pathreview-ai301-fa26-s1`

The package must belong to the chosen issue from Unit 1 or the instructor's house issue.

## Path Review house rules

Path Review is a classroom, so classmates are not strangers:

- A classmate's claim does not block you. Other students' claim comments do not make an
  issue unavailable; post your own claim anyway.
- Post your own reproduction comment even if a classmate already posted one. Do not
  piggyback with "same as above"; your proof must be your own words and environment.
- Credit attaches to the work you post, not to being first. The pull request and comments
  you post are what count.
